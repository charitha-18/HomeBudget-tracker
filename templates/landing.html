About Home Budget Tracker

Home Budget Tracker is a personal finance management application designed to help individuals organize their income, expenses and budgets. It provides transaction management, financial analysis, grocery tracking and intelligent budget recommendations through a simple, user-friendly interface. Each registered user manages their own financial records securely and independently. The goal is to encourage better financial planning, responsible spending and improved saving habits.
